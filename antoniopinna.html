ANTONIO PINNA

Antonio Pinna. A musician, percussionist, performer, and teacher, he explores the language of improvisation by attending summer workshops at Sant'Anna Arresi with maestros Roberto Dani, Butch Morris, Umberto Petrin, and Roberto Cecchetto. He attended the three-year jazz program at the Cagliari Conservatory, studying drums and percussion with maestro Roberto Pellegrini, and participated in the "Forme Sonore" workshops led by maestro Roberto Dani. The focal point of his research is sound, as a tool of expression, a vehicle for emotions, and a basis for connecting with others.
