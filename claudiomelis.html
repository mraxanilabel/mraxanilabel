CLAUDIO MELIS

Claudio Melis is a Sardinian singer-songwriter, composer, and multi-instrumentalist. Born and raised on the island, he began studying music and writing songs, prose, and poetry at a young age. His sound blends the depth of Italian songwriting with classical influences, acoustic and folk traditions, and contemporary pop. Driven by a constant inner, spiritual, and aesthetic search, Claudio offers fragments of real life-intimate, poetic, and multidimensional. On stage, his charismatic presence bridges the tragic and the comic, revealing the full spectrum of human emotion through his music. His world is melancholic yet alive, experimental yet grounded. Claudio Melis is an artist who reshapes sound into meaning, bringing a beautiful sense of playful disorder to silence and introspection.
