LAURA MURA

Since the age of four, she has been making music, but it’s far from her only focus.

What drives her most are the branches of philosophy, psychology, somatics, and science that can be applied to thrive in life.

Beyond writing and performing, she teaches lucid dreaming as a practice for rewiring perception and dissolving mental blocks, works in film as an actress, and brings her creative vision to life through design, photography, and craftsmanship.
